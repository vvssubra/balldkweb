BALLA DK R.I.S.E. GATED CONTENT QUESTIONS
==========================================

ANSWER SCALE FOR ALL SCORING QUESTIONS
0 = Not in place
1 = Starting
2 = Mostly in place
3 = Strong and reviewed

ROUTING QUESTION
What needs to R.I.S.E. next?
A. My finances and family protection
B. My income or career
C. My leadership and team systems

PATH A - R.I.S.E. FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORECARD

R - RESTORE
1. Emergency savings can cover essential expenses.
2. Protection needs are understood and recently reviewed.
3. High-interest debt and monthly cashflow have a clear plan.

I - INCOME
4. Income has a realistic growth plan.
5. There is a backup or second-income strategy.
6. Skills are being developed to increase earning capacity.

S - SUSTAIN
7. Saving or investing happens consistently.
8. Investments match goals, time horizon and risk comfort.
9. Progress is reviewed at least every six months.

E - EMPIRE
10. Nominees, will or estate arrangements are understood.
11. Family can locate key financial information.
12. A long-term legacy, leadership or continuity plan exists.

PATH B - R.I.S.E. CAREER GROWTH SCORECARD

R - READINESS
1. I can manage a learning period without relying on instant income.
2. I understand that licensing, effort and compliance are required.
3. My current financial commitments are visible and manageable.

I - INCOME SKILLS
4. I am open to performance-based income.
5. I am willing to learn communication, prospecting and service.
6. I want a path where income can grow with skill and contribution.

S - SUSTAINABILITY
7. I can follow a weekly activity plan consistently.
8. I respond constructively to coaching and feedback.
9. I prefer long-term skill-building over quick-win hype.

E - EMPIRE POTENTIAL
10. I want to help other people grow.
11. I am interested in leadership and team systems.
12. I want to build impact beyond my own personal production.

SCORING METHOD
- Each category has three questions.
- Category raw score: 0 to 9.
- Normalised score: raw score divided by 9, multiplied by 100.
- 0-33: Needs Attention
- 34-66: Developing
- 67-100: Strong
- The lowest normalised category becomes the likely missing letter.
- If two categories tie, use the selected goal, urgency and chosen path as the tie-break.

SAFETY AND ROUTING RULES
- A recruit with a low Readiness score receives education and a readiness checklist, not a high-pressure career booking.
- Only show CTAs relevant to the visitor's declared path and result.
- Do not ask for identity numbers, salary, policy numbers, medical details, bank information or other unnecessary sensitive data inside the scorecard.
- The scorecard is educational and is not a financial suitability assessment, product recommendation, employment decision or guarantee of results.
