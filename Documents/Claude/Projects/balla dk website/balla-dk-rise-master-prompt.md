## 7. FINANCIAL HEALTH SCORECARD

12 questions. Four-point scoring system:

| Score | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| 0 | Not in place |
| 1 | Weak or inconsistent |
| 2 | Partly in place |
| 3 | Strong and consistent |

### RESTORE — Safety Net

**Q1: If your income stopped today, how long could your emergency savings cover essential expenses?**
- Less than one month = 0
- One to two months = 1
- Three to five months = 2
- Six months or more = 3

**Q2: How confident are you that your current protection would support your family after a serious illness, disability or death?**
- I have no clear protection plan = 0
- I have some coverage but do not know whether it is enough = 1
- I have reviewed most major risks = 2
- I have a recent and structured protection review = 3

**Q3: Do you have updated beneficiary, nomination, will or estate-planning arrangements?**
- Nothing is arranged = 0
- I have thought about it but have not acted = 1
- Some arrangements are in place = 2
- My arrangements are complete and reviewed regularly = 3

### RESTORE — Clean Up

**Q4: How clearly do you understand where your money goes each month?**
- I do not track it = 0
- I check occasionally = 1
- I use a basic monthly budget or tracking method = 2
- I consistently review and optimise my cash flow = 3

**Q5: How manageable are your current debts and monthly commitments?**
- I am missing payments or constantly struggling = 0
- Payments are made, but the pressure is high = 1
- Commitments are manageable with a repayment plan = 2
- Debt is controlled and used strategically = 3

**Q6: At the end of a typical month, what happens to your income?**
- I regularly run short = 0
- Almost everything is used = 1
- I usually save a small amount = 2
- I consistently allocate money toward savings and investments = 3

### INCOME — New Engine

**Q7: How dependent are you on one source of income?**
- Fully dependent on one source = 0
- I have occasional side income = 1
- I have a developing second income stream = 2
- I have multiple reliable income sources = 3

**Q8: How much control do you have over increasing your income during the next 12 months?**
- Very little control = 0
- Some opportunity, but no plan = 1
- I have a realistic growth plan = 2
- I have clear actions, skills and measurable targets = 3

**Q9: How actively are you building skills that can increase your value or earning capacity?**
- I am not currently developing new skills = 0
- I learn occasionally without a clear direction = 1
- I am building one valuable skill = 2
- I follow a structured development and application plan = 3

### SUSTAIN — Money Machine

**Q10: How structured is your current savings and investment plan?**
- I am not saving or investing consistently = 0
- I save when money is available = 1
- I contribute regularly to at least one goal = 2
- I follow a diversified, goal-based long-term plan = 3

**Q11: Do your investments match your goals, time horizon and risk comfort?**
- I do not have investments or do not understand them = 0
- I invested without a clear strategy = 1
- My investments generally match my goals = 2
- My strategy is documented and reviewed regularly = 3

### EMPIRE — Legacy

**Q12: How clear is your long-term plan for retirement, wealth transfer, leadership or legacy?**
- I have no clear plan = 0
- I have ideas but no structure = 1
- I have started planning and taking action = 2
- I have a documented and regularly reviewed legacy plan = 3

## 9. SCORING LOGIC

### Overall Calculation

Maximum score: **36 points**

```
Overall % = (points earned ÷ 36) × 100  [rounded to nearest whole number]
```

### Result Bands

| Band | Score | Meaning |
|------|-------|---------|
| Foundation at Risk | 0–25% | Major gaps need immediate attention. Focus on stability and foundational actions. |
| Building Stability | 26–50% | Some elements exist but the system is inconsistent. Needs structured short-term plan. |
| Growth Ready | 51–75% | Reasonable foundation. Build income, assets or professional momentum. |
| Empire Potential | 76–100% | Strong foundations. Optimise for leadership, systems and legacy. |

### Recommended Next Steps by Band

| Band | Client Recommendation | Recruit Recommendation |
|------|-----------------------|------------------------|
| Foundation at Risk | Book a Financial Foundation Review | Book a Career Readiness Call |
| Building Stability | Receive a 30-Day Restore Plan | Attend a R.I.S.E. Career Preview |
| Growth Ready | Book a R.I.S.E. Growth Strategy Session | Apply for an Agency Opportunity Session |
| Empire Potential | Book a Wealth and Legacy Review | Apply for a Leadership Strategy Session |

## 10. MISSING-LETTER LOGIC

Calculate separate percentage scores for R, I, S, and E. The **lowest percentage** = Missing R.I.S.E. Letter.

When two stages have equal scores, prioritise the earlier foundational stage: **R → I → S → E**

### Missing Letter Results

**Missing R — RESTORE**
- Headline: Your Priority Is RESTORE
- Message: Your next step is to strengthen the foundation before taking on more financial or career complexity.
- Actions: Build/improve emergency buffer · Review protection, debt or financial commitments · Create a 30-day stabilisation plan

**Missing I — INCOME**
- Headline: Your Priority Is INCOME
- Message: Your foundation may be developing, but your income engine needs more attention.
- Actions: Identify an income-growth target · Build one valuable and marketable skill · Explore a realistic second-income or career-development pathway

**Missing S — SUSTAIN**
- Headline: Your Priority Is SUSTAIN
- Message: You are earning, but your money or professional momentum may not be compounding effectively.
- Actions: Define long-term goals · Automate regular savings or development activity · Build a structured growth plan based on time horizon and risk

**Missing E — EMPIRE**
- Headline: Your Priority Is EMPIRE
- Message: You have progress, but the next level requires systems, leadership and a long-term legacy plan.
- Actions: Build repeatable systems · Develop leadership or succession capacity · Document a long-term wealth, team or legacy strategy

## 13. ETHICAL NLP-INSPIRED COPY PRINCIPLES

### Pacing and Leading
Acknowledge reality first, then lead toward the solution.

> *"You may be earning, saving or working hard—and still feel unsure about what comes next. The R.I.S.E. Scorecard helps you identify the next step clearly."*

### Mirroring
Reflect the visitor's own chosen goal in result headings, recommended actions, email subject lines and CTA copy.

### Reframing
> *"You are not failing. You may simply be working on the wrong financial step first."*

### Future Pacing
> *"Imagine entering the next 30 days with one clear priority instead of ten competing financial concerns."*

### Choice Framing
Offer two constructive next steps without false urgency:
- Review my personalised plan
- Speak with a R.I.S.E. adviser

### Autonomy Language
Use: *Explore · Review · Decide · Discover · Compare · Choose your next step*  
Avoid: Coercive language and guaranteed promises.
