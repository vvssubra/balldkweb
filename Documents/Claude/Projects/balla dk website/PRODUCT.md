# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Malaysian individuals and prospective agents landing from ads/social, evaluating whether to trust Balla DK with their finances or career. Two audiences: (1) people wanting financial clarity (safety net, cash flow, income, wealth, legacy), (2) people considering the agency/career opportunity. Mobile-first, short attention span, financial-topic skepticism.

## Product Purpose

One-page R.I.S.E. (Restore, Income, Sustain, Empire) scorecard funnel: visitor takes a free assessment, gets a personalised result, routes to WhatsApp for a human follow-up. No CRM, no email sequence — WhatsApp is the entire conversion mechanism. Success = scorecard completions that convert to WhatsApp conversations.

## Brand Personality

No-nonsense mentor. Direct but caring, credible and practical, confident without arrogance, educational rather than hard-selling. Malaysian and culturally relatable. Obsessed with systems, clarity, action, accountability — not hype. Language is plain Malaysian English, jargon-free.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-SaaS template look (gradient text, glassmorphism cards, hero-metric blocks, identical icon+heading+text card grids)
- Cheap stock finance photography, fake currency graphics, generic finance imagery
- Aggressive countdown timers, coercive language, guaranteed-outcome promises
- Excessive gradients, flashy/bouncy animation
- Overloaded dashboards

## Design Principles

- Trust through restraint: whitespace and clear hierarchy read as credible; visual noise reads as a scam funnel.
- Show the system, don't sell the sizzle: the R.I.S.E. framework itself is the hero, not stock imagery or manufactured urgency.
- Dark premium sections for authority moments (hero, CTA), light sections for reading/comprehension — contrast is structural, not decorative.
- Every visual choice should feel like it came from a Malaysian financial mentor's actual practice, not a generic template.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG 2.1 AA. Never communicate score/status by colour alone (pair with icon/text) — required given R/I/S/E colour-coded scoring. Respect prefers-reduced-motion. Visible focus states throughout (scorecard flow is a critical conversion path).
